# Asset structure

- Start with one asset for the whole equipment.
- Only add a sub-asset if you will track work on it at that level, such as raising work orders against it. Typical reasons:
    - it has its own maintenance plan, contractor or calibration
    - it has its own serial number or can be swapped between machines
    - its own failure history or downtime is worth tracking
- Everything else (filters, batteries, seals, buttons, covers, chargers, holders) stays in the parent and goes to the bill of materials.
- Keep it to two or three levels at most.

Use your judgement from the manual's maintenance and troubleshooting sections. Small equipment is usually just one asset.
